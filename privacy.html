Policy

Privacy and policy

Grad Connect Uganda publishes graduate career information and opportunity guides.

Information on the site

Opportunity details are checked against their source before publication. Sources and dates are shown with each item.

Data protection

Personal details are handled in line with the Uganda Data Protection and Privacy Act 2019. Personal details are not published on the site.

Contact

Enquiries are handled on the Contact Us page.

Accessibility

Semantic headings, descriptive links, labelled fields, visible focus, high-contrast colours and alt text are used throughout.
