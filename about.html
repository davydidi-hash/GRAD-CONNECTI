About the platform

About Grad Connect Uganda

Graduate opportunities, profile tools, employer tools and published evidence in one place.

Purpose and background

Why this site exists

UBOS 2024 census records 12.3% unemployment among people aged 15 and above and 4,001,528 (42.6%) youth aged 15–24 not in employment, education or training (1). The Kampala profile records 87,468 unemployed people (13.3%) and 31.9% NEET among 15–24-year-olds (2).

Twaweza 2025 reports 49% of Greater Kampala 18–29-year-olds naming unemployment among the top three national problems (3). State House 2024 reports a UBOS graduate unemployment figure of 15.2%, while expert commentary ranges up to 80% (4). Daily Monitor 2025 reports Mastercard Foundation figures of 12.86% formal graduate employment, over 700,000 graduates a year and about 90,000 formal jobs (5).

Published evidence

What the platform offers

The website presents freelance jobs, internships and short projects, explains application steps, gives CV and safety guidance, and shows how employers submit vacancies for checking.
