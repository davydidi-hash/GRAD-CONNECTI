How to use the site

CV writing and interviews

Avoiding scams

Offers requesting payment, passwords or bank details should be reported.

Questions

Common Questions

How do I apply?

Open Opportunities, choose View details, then use Apply. The application page keeps the job reference.

Can I add a profile?

Yes. The Graduate page has profile fields, a resume upload, interests, skills and a profile image field.

Can employers see applications?

Yes. The Employer page includes an application table with shortlist checkboxes.

How do I post a vacancy?

Open the Employer page and complete the vacancy form with the role, location, requirements and closing date.
